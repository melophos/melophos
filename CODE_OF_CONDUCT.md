# Code of Conduct

## Standards

MELOPHOS is for musicians and builders at every level, from someone lighting their first scale to someone designing a new board. Anyone taking part through issues, pull requests or discussions is expected to be respectful and patient with beginners, give specific feedback rather than vague criticism and assume good intent.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, abuse or personal attacks of any kind. Spam or off-topic promotion. Sharing anyone's private information without consent. Posting material that infringes someone else's copyright, including sharing copyrighted songs or transcriptions.

## Scope

This code applies to every MELOPHOS repository and issue and pull request and discussion and to the MELOPHOS community Discord server. It also applies when someone represents MELOPHOS in public.

## Reporting

- **On GitHub:** email [contact@melophos.com](mailto:contact@melophos.com) with links to what happened.
- **On the Discord server:** use the report option on a message or type `/report`. For an account matter or a privacy matter or a conversation you would rather keep private, type `/ticket`. Only you and the moderators can read it.
- **About a moderator or maintainer:** write to [contact@melophos.com](mailto:contact@melophos.com) instead of using `/report` or `/ticket`.
- **Security problems:** never use a report or a ticket. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

We aim to look at every report within two days. Nobody is on call so a weekend can take longer. Reports are handled in confidence and only the people who need to know see them.

We start with a reminder. After that we may remove content or pause someone's ability to post or block them from a repository or remove them from the server. Serious harm skips the first steps.

To question a decision write to [contact@melophos.com](mailto:contact@melophos.com). A different person looks at it where that is possible.
