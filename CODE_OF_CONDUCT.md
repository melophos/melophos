# Code of Conduct

## Standards

MELOPHOS is for musicians and builders at every level, from someone lighting their first scale to someone designing a new board. Anyone taking part through issues, pull requests or discussions is expected to be respectful and patient with beginners, give specific feedback rather than vague criticism and assume good intent.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, abuse or personal attacks of any kind. Spam or off-topic promotion. Sharing anyone's private information without consent. Posting material that infringes someone else's copyright, including sharing copyrighted songs or transcriptions.

## Enforcement

> [!NOTE]
> Report unacceptable behaviour to contact@melophos.com. Reports are reviewed and appropriate action is taken, including removing content or blocking users where necessary.
