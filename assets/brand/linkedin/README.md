## Tagline

Song made visible. An open instrument learning platform that lights the notes on any keyboard or guitar.

## About

MELOPHOS is an open instrument learning platform. An ESP32-S3 hub lights the notes to play on any keyboard or guitar, logs every note you play and scores your practice on a self-hosted server.

We built it for learners who want clear, immediate feedback without a subscription or a closed app. Lights show what comes next, practice history shows how you are improving and everything runs on hardware and software you control.

The name joins two Ancient Greek roots: melos means song or melody. Phos means light. Together they make song made visible.

MELOPHOS is developed in the open on GitHub.
